## Conventions seen in the images

- **Grid (16 cells, ring)**: 1 = Warden's room (top-left corner, never holds a prisoner); top 2→6 left to right; right side 7, 8 (top to bottom); bottom 9→14 right to left; left side 15, 16 (bottom to top). In other words, clockwise numbering. [27, 31, 38, 41]
- **Frame/aura color = rarity**: white (Normal), blue (Magic), purple (Rare), orange (Legendary), red (Ultimate / 至臻), rainbow flame (Chromatic / 绮彩). White/blue/orange confirmed by VOD captions ("white-rarity Mammoth", "upgrade to blue", "orange Mammoth"); "Ultimate" in red in the objective [03/41]; purple and red by elimination. - **Red fangs icon over the portrait** = "has a target it can consume" (VOD caption in 27).

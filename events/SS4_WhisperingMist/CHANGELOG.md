## 2026-10-17 — event added to the base (retrospective research)

Written two and a half years after launch, from archived and current sources. What the history says:

**Official**
- 2024-03-29: season teaser; preview stream on 2024-04-13 (EN patch notes the same day).
- 2024-04-18 19:00 PDT (2024-04-19 10:00 GMT+8): launch on global and CN at once, with the 1st anniversary. CN name 「雾都怪谈」.
- 2024-04-21 (XD CN, TapTap): first 10 days of Mistville made easier; Mistville Residents' damage −30%; Mist Essence made tradeable (it was a bug); a Mistville wall that gave some builds huge damage reduction fixed.
- 2024-07-04 (SS5 Clockwork Ballet notes, published 2024-07-01): Mistville becomes a core mode. Netherrealm drops only Resident's Eye, with less Infection Progress per Eye; Residents spawn without the rift portal; Mist Essence drop rate ×2; Deduction Record costs −66% (tier 1) / −50% (others), tier 2/3 gates 15/20 points; folklore fights eased; Activation Medium drops defined per month; sanity confirm prompt; all Old Stuff and Food values rebalanced; Month 7 slightly harder.
- No English hotfix touching Mistville found between 2024-04-18 and 2024-07-04.

**Measured live (community)**
- 2024-04/05: entry ~300 Infection Progress per run at 7-1, cap 900, ~20 Infection Progress per Resident's Eye (CALC from "45 eyes is 900"); Month 7 needed 42 Mist Essence; ~30 Sanity per cell by day 11–12.
- 2024-05: 300–400 FE per completion at 8-4; 80%+ day-15 rate claimed by experienced players.
- 2024-09: ~70% clear rate (one CN player, no pet refresh).
- 2025-04: TapTap poll n=95, 48.4% think the rewards are reasonable; Month 7 is where the profit is.
- 2025-11: Mist Essence 80–100 FE.

**Our corrections**
- tlidb's EN text "July Whispering Mist gameplay" means **Month 7** (第七月), a difficulty tier, not the month of July.
- The CN server did not run this season earlier: CN and global launched it the same day.
